GET TO KNOW TAXMAN

Real people. Personal service.
Experienced tax preparation.

TAXMAN is operated by Randal L. Burnie, B.Comm. and has served Halifax Regional Municipality and Nova Scotia since 2002. We help individuals, self-employed people, and incorporated businesses prepare and file tax returns with confidence.

From everyday T1 returns to T2 corporate filings, bookkeeping, payroll and HST, we focus on practical, personalized assistance and accurate preparation. You can also ask about preparing your return without an in-person visit.

Our specialized work includes final (Terminal T1) returns for deceased people, optional Rights or Things returns and executor-related tax guidance. We understand that these matters require both care and attention to detail.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions before you get started?

Here are a few practical answers. For your specific tax situation, contact TAXMAN.

Do I have to visit the office for a T1 return? +

Not necessarily. TAXMAN promotes no-visit personal tax preparation. Contact the office to discuss the current document submission process.

Can TAXMAN help my incorporated business? +

Yes. TAXMAN advertises T2 corporate returns, bookkeeping, payroll and HST reporting services.

What if I am an executor handling someone's estate? +

Ask about final T1 returns, optional Rights or Things returns, clearance certificate guidance. Your requirements depend on the estate's circumstances.

How can I get in touch? +
